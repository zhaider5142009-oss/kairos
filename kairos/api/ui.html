Doctrine: paper-first, fail-closed. Every open passes plan-check (R:R, risk-per-trade, confidence, model agreement) and the risk gate; every held position is actively managed — hard stops, targets, a trailing ratchet in R-multiples, venue reconciliation. Circuit breakers pause entries on a day past the loss brake — the tighter of risk.daily_loss_limit_pct and rules.max_daily_loss_pct, whichever key the config sets — at 60% gross exposure, or four straight losses; exits are never rate-limited. A live session never resurrects after a restart — you must re-arm it consciously.
